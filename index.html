Prototype notes

A focused first version.

This demo helps validate the proposal workflow. It is not yet a production SaaS account or payment system.

Does this send a proposal to a customer?

No. It currently lets you preview, copy, export and print a proposal. Live email delivery and secure shareable links are future features.

Where are saved drafts stored?

Drafts stay in this browser using local storage. They are not synced to an online account or shared across devices.

Are the example prices industry recommendations?

No. All company details, costs and prices are sample values. Contractors must enter their own verified costs, prices and scope.

Can I charge customers for this version?

Not yet. Authentication, cloud storage, payment processing, data protection, terms and production security need to be implemented and tested first.
